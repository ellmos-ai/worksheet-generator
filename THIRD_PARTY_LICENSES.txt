1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ZERO-COPYLEFT ISOLATION GUARANTEE
================================================================================

worksheet-generator is an unprivileged, local-first educational material generator for
special education professionals, therapists, and classroom teachers. It adheres to strict
zero-egress, privacy-first runtime principles.

- Zero Mandatory Dependencies (INV-ZERODEP-03): The entire core generator, schema validator,
  curriculum adapter, and Markdown/HTML renderers run exclusively on the Python 3.10+
  Standard Library.
- Zero-Copyleft Isolation Guarantee (INV-PERMISSIVE-06): No GPL, AGPL, SSPL, or viral copyleft
  dependencies are incorporated into the runtime distribution. All direct, optional, and
  development dependencies are strictly permissive (MIT, PSF-2.0).
- Unprivileged User-Mode (RunAsInvoker, INV-UNPRIV-02): worksheet-generator executes
  exclusively in standard, non-elevated user space without requesting administrative rights.
- Output Ownership Guarantee (INV-OUTPUT-07): All generated worksheets (JSON, Markdown,
  HTML, DOCX) remain the 100% exclusive intellectual property of the generating user.
